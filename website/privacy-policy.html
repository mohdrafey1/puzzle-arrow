Puzzle Arrow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2025

Puzzle Arrow ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source puzzle game developed by Mohd Rafey. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Puzzle Arrow is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data to support game features:

  • Game Progress: Your level progress, achievements, and customizations are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
  • Community Levels: When you publish or play community levels, basic data (level data, play counts, ratings) is stored on Firebase.
  • Leaderboard Data: If you participate in leaderboards, your display name and scores are stored on Firebase.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain device information and advertising identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your preferences. You can learn more about Google's data practices at Google Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): For community levels, leaderboards, and real-time database. Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements. AdMob Privacy

Data Storage

Most game data (progress, settings, unlocked items) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. Community-related data is stored securely on Firebase servers.

Children's Privacy

Puzzle Arrow does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not require account creation or personal information to play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us
