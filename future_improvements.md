# Future Improvements

## Core Gameplay Polish

- Add satisfying sounds/haptics when placing an arrow, deleting an arrow, or when the arrows start moving during playtesting.
- Implement Daily Challenges: Feature one specific curated community level every day for all players to compete on (fastest time or least moves).
- Smooth Animations: Instead of arrows instantly resolving, animate them moving from tile to tile using `react-native-reanimated` for a much more premium feel.

## Progression Systems

- Unlockable Customization: As players beat levels (or earn an in-game currency), allow them to unlock different colored arrows, board themes, or custom confetti styles.
- Achievements/Badges: Simple badges on the profile for "Beat 10 Community Levels", "Created Your First Level", etc.
